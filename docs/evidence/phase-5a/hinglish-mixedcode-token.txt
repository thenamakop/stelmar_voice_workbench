Decision (PROVISIONAL; Phase 5 If Blocked rule): the <|mixedcode|> token is lost in conversion. On these two
clips the trimmed model decodes (Devanagari Hindi; some English words stay in Latin script) where the raw one
crashes, so it is kept for evaluation through --file. Two clips do not show its output is "still correct":
the keep-or-drop decision rests on the 20-clip CoSHE-Eval smoke runs (smoke-hinglish-q8_0-coshe and
smoke-hinglish-q5_0-coshe beside smoke-medium-coshe, current role settings; WER, deletions and the
Latin->Devanagari rate in tables-phase-5a.md) and finally on Phase 5's full runs. whisper.cpp cannot prompt or produce <|mixedcode|>, so D14 (English words in Latin script)
depends on what the model writes without it; Phase 5 measures that on CoSHE-Eval and the local mixed set
(Latin->Devanagari rate) and records D14's fallback if it falls short. Never load the raw file: it crashes
whisper.cpp v1.9.5.

5. 20-clip CoSHE-Eval smoke runs, VAD chunks of at most 15 s (valid results; 9 Nov 2026)
   An earlier run of these three decoded each 56 s clip in 30 s windows without model timestamps; whisper.cpp
   stops such a window at its token limit and skips the rest, losing 20 to 41% of the reference words. Those
   numbers were withdrawn after the re-review. The harness now cuts each clip at VAD pauses (whisper.cpp's
   Silero VAD, eval/wbeval/chunking.py) into chunks of at most 15 s and decodes each chunk on its own.
   Machine: Legion (native, CPU only). whisper.cpp v1.9.5 (d1be6fde11ac), portable AVX2 build, 8 threads,
   night role (beam 5, fallback, previous-chunk context, VAD, no model timestamps), language given from the
   reference (oracle: detection not measured). Run through eval/run.py all; results in eval/results/TABLES.md,
   copied to tables-phase-5a.md. The longest chunk was 14.1 to 14.8 s, depending on the run.
