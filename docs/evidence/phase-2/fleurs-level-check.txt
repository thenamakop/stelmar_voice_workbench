ANALYSIS (11 Nov 2026):
  The eval/data WAVs equal the original audio in the Hugging Face cache to within 0.02 dB in all 1,364 clips of FLEURS en, hi and ur: eval/data is not mis-scaled and eval/get_data.py is not changed. The quietness is in the source recordings: 451 of 647 English test clips have an RMS under -50 dBFS (median -60.0 dBFS), against a median of -31.5 dBFS in Hindi and -38.1 dBFS in Urdu (and -21.2 dBFS in CoSHE-Eval).
  So no committed result used mis-scaled audio. Every committed FLEURS result (Phase 5a's baselines, the 3-WER comparison and parity in docs/evidence/phase-2/wer-*.txt) used the audio as the source has it. The parity comparison feeds whisper-cli and wb-engine the same files and is unaffected. The absolute English FLEURS error rates are the rates on that quiet source; they are not evidence about the same speech at a normal level, and a level effect on them has not been measured.
  Where level matters, normalisation is done in the test-clip builder only (scripts/gates/codeswitch.py builds the control at -26 dBFS RMS); eval/data and the harness are unchanged.
