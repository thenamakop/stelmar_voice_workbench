Notes for Phase 3 (owner, 11 Nov 2026):
(a) The Intel UHD of the Legion is weaker than the integrated GPUs the room laptops are likely to have, so every UHD row is a FLOOR: a laptop with Iris Xe, Arc or a Radeon 680M/780M is expected to be at least as fast. The exception is the low-baseline laptop (basic integrated graphics): it may be SLOWER than the Legion UHD, so for it these rows are an estimate to be re-measured there, not a bound.
(b) Hindi costs more to decode than English on the same hardware: in these tables the Hindi clip runs 1.3 to 4.4 times slower than the English clip (about 3 times for large-v3 q5_0 on the UHD: 0.51 against about 1.5). The two clips are different recordings, so part of the gap belongs to the clip. Phase 3 tiers must consider turbo or medium for Hindi on weak machines.
(c) On the UHD, DXGI reports the integrated GPU's memory as local (the shared figure stays 0.00). That memory is system RAM: Phase 3 counts it as RAM (AGENTS.md: integrated GPUs share RAM with the CPU).
