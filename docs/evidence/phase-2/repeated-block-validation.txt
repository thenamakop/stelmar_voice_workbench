FINDING: the shared upstream breaks are not repeated blocks.
  The gate evidence and docs/PLAN.md section 12 call the shared breaks "repeated blocks" because the harness reports a negative source jump of about one WASAPI period ("repeat 10.2 ms").
  At the sample level every one of them is a run of exact digital zeros of 490 to 551 frames (10.2 to 11.5 ms) inserted into the noise signal: the recording holds 537 zero frames at app audio 287,916.9 ms in the load run, 490 at 2,346.6 ms in the panic run, and so on. The negative jump is the delay those inserted frames add to the recording.
  Neither the player, the app nor the reference capture can tell it from the signal; all three record the zeros identically. No exact repeat (a stretch equal to the one before it at a lag of 2.7 to 100 ms) exists in any recording scanned.
  So the recorder has two detectors: repeated_block (an exact repeat; unit-tested on spliced noise, found nowhere in the real recordings) and zero_gap (a run of 1 to 50 ms of exact zeros in every channel with live audio on both sides; found at every shared break).

detectors (the code the recorder thread runs; crates/wb-recorder/src/repeat.rs and dropout.rs):
  repeated_block: a stretch of at least 256 frames equal sample for sample to the stretch lag frames before it (lag 128 to 4,800), RMS at least -60 dBFS, run length at most 2 x lag (longer is a periodic signal)
  zero_gap: 1 to 50 ms of frames in which every channel is exactly 0.0, with at least half the frames not exactly zero in the 10 ms before and the 10 ms after
